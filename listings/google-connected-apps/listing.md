# Igdrasil — console copy

Draft for the Google Connected Console. Not submitted. The product is Swedish-only. Swedish is the primary locale. The English lines are optional, for a field that also asks for English.

Paste the text inside each block, including the locale tags. Each block is one console field.

Read-only. The copy covers results and balance, the VAT position, and unpaid customer invoices, following `skills/monthly-finance-review`, `skills/swedish-vat-review`, and `skills/open-invoice-review`.

## Name

Igdrasil

This is the display name and the `@` handle.
